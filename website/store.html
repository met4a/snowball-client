What you get

Cape Studio

Paint the outside of your cape one pixel at a time, right in the game: Right Shift, Cosmetics, Cape Studio. Draw up to four frames and it animates on your back. Staff look at each design before it shows to other Snowball players.

Three Snowball+ capes

Glacier, Comet and Prism, next to the eight free ones. Every Snowball player sees them on your back.

Name colour

Pick one of seven colours for your name on your Snowball nametag and in the launcher's chat, under Cosmetics.

Four more emotes

Snowball Throw, Flex, Victory and Float, on the third page of the emote wheel (B). Everyone with Snowball sees you do them.

The Snowball+ badge

Your snowball in Tab and above your head turns into the Snowball+ one while you play with Snowball Client, and your messages in the launcher's chat carry a Snowball+ tag.

How it works

Three steps

  1. 1Type the Minecraft name that should get Snowball+ and press Subscribe.
  2. 2Pay on Stripe's checkout. Snowball+ arrives on that account within a minute; nothing to install.
  3. 3It renews every month. Cancel any time at link.com, signing in with the email you paid with; Snowball+ stays until the month you paid for runs out.

Questions

About Snowball+

Who takes the payment?

Stripe sells Snowball+ through Link, its checkout, which takes the payment and handles sales tax, receipts and refund requests. Your bank statement shows LINK.COM. Your card details never reach Snowball; Snowball's server only hears which Minecraft account paid and whether the subscription is still running.

I paid but I do not have Snowball+

Check the name you typed at checkout: Snowball+ goes to that account. If it is right, restart the launcher, then ask staff in the launcher's chat and say when you paid.

What happens to my cape if I cancel?

When Snowball+ ends, your custom cape comes off and the Cape Studio closes. Your approved design is kept: subscribe again and you can wear it straight away.

Does Snowball+ give an advantage in game?

No. It is cosmetic. Every module that changes how you play is in the free client.
